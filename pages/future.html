03 · look forward

There is still so much life.

Not a giant plan. Just little doors you might like to open someday.

☼

What would you love to experience for the first time?

For a free afternoon

Walk somewhere you have never walked. Take photos of ordinary things. Buy a snack you have never tried.

For a difficult evening

Shower. Change the sheets. Put on something familiar. Make tea. Let the evening be small.

For a future morning

Wake up early once. See the sky change. You don't need a reason beyond being there.
