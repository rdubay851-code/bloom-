06 · about bloom

A small place to land.

Bloom was made around a simple idea: when someone is having a difficult day, the internet can contain one small corner that asks nothing from them.

Nothing to perform.

You can visit for thirty seconds or an hour. There is no streak you can fail and no mood you have to reach.

Make the atmosphere yours.

Soft Light, Night Blue and Morning Mist are remembered on this device so you can choose what feels right.

Private by default.

The journal is stored locally in your browser in this version. It is not uploaded to a server.

For real support too.

If sadness becomes overwhelming or you feel unsafe, a trusted person or qualified mental-health professional can offer something a website cannot: real human support.
